# Slides

The slide deck view of a note inside one framed shell: a rail column of slide titles with the current slide's subsections, the deck, and a control bar with a segmented ruler. Two modes sit on top: an overview grid and a fullscreen presentation.

**Source:** `quartz/components/SlidesContent.tsx` · styles: `quartz/components/styles/slides.scss` · script: `quartz/components/scripts/slides.inline.ts` · data: `quartz/plugins/transformers/slides.ts` · pages: `quartz/plugins/emitters/slidesPage.tsx`

## Preview

A copy of the server-rendered markup from `/personality/slides/1` (selector `.slides-root`), styled by `site/garden.css`, the site's compiled CSS pruned to the rules these previews use. On the site a page carries the current slide only; here all seven slide bodies are inlined and marked loaded, so the script never fetches. Links point to `#`. The preview is interactive: it calls `Garden.mount()` from `components/bundle.js`, which runs the site's own script on this markup. Fullscreen is not available inside the preview frame, so the present button stays hidden there.

## Rules

- Pages: each slide is its own page at `<note>/slides/<n>`, so a slide has an address and the pager works before the script. The old `<note>/slides` address redirects to `slides/1`. The script turns slides in place: it fetches the slide's fragment (`static/slides/<note>/<hash>`), mounts it, and replaces the URL; the neighbours are fetched ahead. A fragment that fails to load falls back to the slide's page.
- Reading workspace: the page owns `100dvh`, hides the header and footer, and pads the content like the flashcards shell (`clamp(0.75rem, 4dvh, 2.5rem) 1rem`). The root fills that area as one `line`-bordered frame on the page colour. Inside it the rail is a column with a `line` separator toward the deck; the control bar runs across the whole frame beneath both, inset `--space-4` from the frame edge. The deck scrolls on its own; slide content keeps the prose defaults.
- The rail and controls use the tool recipe under the `slides-` prefix: `--slides-line` is `line`, `--slides-surface` is `surface`. Controls are `1.5rem`, 44px under `pointer: coarse`.
- Focus: the table-of-contents label (`.slides-toc-label`) takes the ring on the child, because the focusable wrapper is not the visible box (see `focus.md`, item 2).
- Slides split on headings. The transformer also records the headings nested under each slide; the rail shows one nesting level (the shallowest present) under the current slide only. The script sets each sublist's height, so the closing and opening lists move together, and the subsections settle in one after another behind the opening box.
- Markers are squares; depth shows as size. A slide is a `0.4rem` square, hollow (`gray` border on the page colour) ahead of the reader and filled `dark` once reached. A subsection is a `0.25rem` filled square in `gray`, `dark` while its heading is at the top of the deck. The rail line is one segment per entry, from its square to the next, so it follows an open sublist without measurement. Segments above the current slide are `dark`; the current segment fills as far as the deck has scrolled through the slide.
- Rail items are `ui` in `darkgray`; subsection items are `meta` in `darkgray`. The current slide is `dark` with `aria-current="step"`; the current subsection is `dark` with `aria-current="location"`. Nothing is italic.
- The rail carries no title or count. Its header holds two `1.5rem` controls at the top-left of the frame: the rail toggle (three bars, `aria-expanded`, "hide rail" / "show rail") and then the home link (house icon, "back to note"). Collapsed, the rail column shrinks to those two controls and the list is hidden; the choice is kept in `localStorage` (`slides-rail-collapsed`) and an inline script restores it before first paint. The readout ("1 / 7"), the overview captions ("3 · title") and the timer ("04:12") are `meta-mono` with tabular figures.
- Ruler: the control bar's top edge is a 2px grid with one segment per slide and 2px gaps, `line` ahead and `dark` through the current slide. It spans the whole bar. Segment widths follow the slide's text length (1fr per 400 characters, minimum 1fr), so the ruler shows where the deck's bulk is. It stays `role="progressbar"`.
- Overview (`o`, the four-squares button): every fragment mounts and the deck becomes a grid of 4:3 cards. The script picks the fewest columns whose rows still fit the deck, so the cards are as large as the space allows; past that the grid scrolls, with cards no narrower than `14rem`. Rows are `max-content`: the deck's height is fixed by the frame, and auto rows would shrink to share it. Each card shows the slide's body scaled by a transform at the ratio of the card's inner width to the slide's width (so its size never feeds the grid rows) inside a `line` border, with a page-coloured fade over the clipped bottom. The current card carries a `dark` border and caption. Arrows move (up and down by a row), Enter or a click picks, Escape leaves. The toggle button shows `aria-pressed`, filled `dark` on `light` while on.
- Present (`p`, the four-corners button): fullscreen on `.slides-root`, which drops its frame border. The rail and buttons hide, the deck centres at `60rem` with `zoom: 1.25`, the ruler sits on the bottom edge, and the readout (with the timer when running) sits in the bottom-right corner. The button only renders where the Fullscreen API exists.
- Controls: the readout sits at the start of the bar; at the end sit the two mode buttons and then the previous/next pair, adjacent so they read as one pager. The pair are links (`./n`) with `data-router-ignore`; past either end a link loses its `href` and reads `role="link" aria-disabled="true"` at opacity 0.5, and focus moves to the other one. Buttons and links carry inline `1.5` stroke icons (square caps) centred in the control box, and `title` hints that name the key: "overview (o)", "present (p)", "previous slide (←)", "next slide (→)".
- Keys: ← → and space turn slides (shift+space goes back), Home and End jump to the ends, digits typed within 700ms jump to that slide, `t` toggles an elapsed timer. The deck's rows join the site's shortcut panel (⌘') while the page is open. The vim keys the site already claims (f, j, k, g, G, D, H, M, L) stay untouched.
- Rail links carry `data-router-ignore`; the deck handles them. A click on a subsection of another slide lands on that heading at once; within the current slide it scrolls there smoothly (instantly under reduced motion).
- Touch: a horizontal swipe of 48px or more on the deck turns the slide (`touch-action: pan-y pinch-zoom`). A gesture that starts inside a horizontally scrolling element (a table, a code block) is left to that element.
- Hover, fine pointers only: a rail item turns `dark` and underlines; a button fills with `surface` and its border rises to `gray`; an overview card fills with `surface`.
- Focus: `2px solid focus`, offset 2px. On a rail item the ring sits on the label span, so it hugs the text inside the masked scroller.
- Motion: 150ms on `color`, `background-color`, `border-color`; the rail fill and the sublists move at the panel timing (220ms); a slide enters with a 220ms fade and a `--space-1` rise. Reduced motion stops all of it.
- Print shows every slide, one per page, without the rail or the controls.
- Accessible names are lowercase: "back to note", "hide rail", "show rail", "previous slide", "next slide", "overview", "present", "slides progress", "slide controls".
