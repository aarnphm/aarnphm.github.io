# Dropcap

The initial that opens a lede or a chapter on a reading page, and the specimen card that shows one face on a note page. The faces follow gwern.net's drop caps.

**Source:** `quartz/styles/pages/newspaper/dropcap.scss` · markup: `quartz/util/dropcap.ts`, `quartz/plugins/transformers/profile/initials.ts` · fonts: `harness/fonts/subset/initials.py`, ink tables in `quartz/styles/fonts.initials.scss` · procedural tile: `quartz/util/grapevine/presets.ts`

## Inputs

- Frontmatter `dropcap: <face>`. Newspaper pages get `garamond` with no frontmatter. Any other layout gets an initial only when `dropcap` is set. `none` turns them off. An unknown name falls back to `garamond`.
- A ` ```dropcap ` fence with `type=<face>` and, for `procedural`, `seed=<id>`. Its first letter becomes a specimen card (`figure.dropcap-specimen`) with the initial over a caption. The fence renders on the note page and in the latent reader's passages.

Each monochrome face is served one letter per file from the site's font store, so each letter has its own family and the line strut uses its metrics. The system does not carry these fonts; the preview uses the fallback serif.

## Rules

- The letter's ink, not its em, spans the lines: from the cap top of the first line to the baseline of the last. `--dc-block` is `(lines - 1) × line + cap`; `--dc-size` is the block divided by the face's ink span (`--dc-top - --dc-bottom`, in em from the baseline); `--dc-lift` moves the float so the ink starts at the first cap top. Every face and letter has its own ink top and bottom in the tables (`$garamond-ink`, `fonts.$initials-ink`), measured by `initials.py`, which also normalises each face's vertical metrics so a `line-height: 1` box ends at the baseline.
- Lengths are rem and px. `em` and `lh` resolve against the initial's own size and break the fit. Newspaper pages set the three line metrics (`--mag-line`, `--mag-cap`, `--mag-cap-top`) for Source Serif 4 in `column.scss`; reading pages match PP Neue Montreal at 1rem on 1.6.
- A lede spans three lines by default; a face may set its own (`yinit`: 4). A paragraph that opens with a quotation mark hangs the mark in the margin so the initial sits flush with the text.
- A specimen card is `inline-flex`, column, centred, 9rem wide, `0 0.5rem 1.5rem 0` margins, so a row of fences reads as a gallery. The initial is a block as tall as `--dc-block` with no float and no margin, translated by `(--dc-top - 1) × --dc-size` so every face's ink starts at the top of the block and the captions share one line. An inline initial takes the glyph's advance and line metrics as its box, which stretches a procedural tile; do not use one.
- The default face's ornament fills its em square (within 0.013em for every letter), about 1.23 times its letter's ink. The specimen fits the square to the block (`--dc-top: 1.139`, `--dc-bottom: 0.15`, measured in the browser), so the ornament stands as tall as the other faces' letters and the caption sits under it with no gap.
- The caption is `meta` in `darkgray` at line height 1.3, 0.4rem under the initial. A named face is captioned by its name (`goudy`). A procedural specimen is captioned by its seed alone, in italics (`<i>621289</i>`): the seed the fence names, else the preset the page resolves to.
- Specimens inherit the reading page's paragraph rules: the host paragraph is `flow-root` so it grows around the float.
