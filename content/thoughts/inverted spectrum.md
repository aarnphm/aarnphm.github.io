the inverted spectrum case asks whether two people could share functional roles while their color experience is swapped. Locke has the early version. Shoemaker makes the modern pressure test. [@locke1689essay; @shoemaker1982invertedspectrum]

the case only works if same function plus same public language leaves phenomenal character open. Hardin presses from color structure. Hue, saturation, brightness, and opponent relations constrain what a color experience can be. [@hardin1988color] Dennett presses the evidential criteria. If every use and discrimination is held fixed, it becomes unclear what evidence remains for the inversion. [@dennett1988quining]

the payoff for this cluster is narrow. Inverted spectrum is a stress test for [[thoughts/functionalism]]. It asks whether functional sameness entails phenomenal sameness.
