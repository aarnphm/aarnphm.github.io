### Motivation.

vLLM has always been a developer-first inference serving framework. Its progress is powered by a global community of contributors who add model support, optimize kernels, expand hardware coverage, strengthen the serving stack, and improve the developer experience.

Coding agents are rapidly becoming a standard part of software development. They help developers navigate unfamiliar codebases, investigate issues, implement changes, write tests, and move from ideas to pull requests faster.

The rise of coding agents gives us an opportunity to accelerate vLLM’s open-source development. By making the repository easier to understand, modify, and validate, we can empower more contributors and build an even better inference engine together.
