# #33339 Enable Cross layers KV cache layout at NIXL Connector V2

https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/33339 · liranschour · MERGED · created 2026-01-29T12:06:30Z · merged 2026-02-05T10:17:03Z

## Purpose

Enable NIXL Connector to us the new continuous cross layer KV cache layout described in [RFC](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/issues/27742) and implemented in [#27743](https://github.com/vllm-project/vllm/pull/27743)

Demonstrate performance improvement of more the 2x in Tok/sec and TTFT due to dramatic reduction of fragmentation of transfer buffers.

Tested with P!=D with run_accuracy_test.sh P=1 D=2
