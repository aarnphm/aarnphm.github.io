## Le Sillon and Zaza

Zaza's father had belonged to [[thoughts/Le Sillon|Le Sillon]], the Catholic democratic movement associated with Marc Sangnier. Beauvoir recalls repeating her father's amusement at an assault on Sangnier's supporters. Zaza objects, and Simone realizes she has borrowed a judgment she cannot defend. The episode connects Zaza's family's sense of Catholic social responsibility with Simone's growing scrutiny of her own inherited opinions. See the [French text](https://livreur2soleil.com/yms/Ebooks/Romans/Simone%20De%20Beavoir/Memoires%20d_une%20jeune%20fille%20rang%20-%20Simone%20de%20Beauvoir.pdf), in the rue Saint-Benoît passage.
