## October 14, 2024, 18:41

It is Canadian Thanksgiving. I have assignments to finish, and I am going to a Friendsgiving at 19:30, hosted by a friend from high school. We attended the same university and still became too busy to see each other.

I am looking forward to the dinner because I want to repair the friendship. The distance may have come from different lives or from a conflict I invented by leaving it unexamined.

Books often help me regain some balance. I picked up John Green's _An Abundance of Katherines_, then left this entry unfinished.

## January 6, 2025, 21:41

The wind is loud outside. I heard an old bed frame creak through the wall. The couple across the balcony had left their shade open. We sometimes leave bottles of wine for each other, and I have invited them over for dinner.

I finished half a mug of coffee while reading Kant's _Groundwork of the Metaphysics of Morals_. I still had project proposals and cleaning to finish.

I had visited Toronto and met H. Her presence made the day feel full. I kept returning to the city because I felt at home there.

I had also ended a relationship with someone I met during a summer visit to Montréal. I travelled there again two or three weeks earlier. The distance exposed different lives and intentions. She was doing her thing. I was doing mine.

Tranquility that night meant letting both facts remain true. Toronto felt like home, and my home was still here.
