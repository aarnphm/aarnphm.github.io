_the first draft of this note began with an equation that could only return the answer i had put into it._

i assigned high values to distance, shared language, bodily ease, support, and low possessive pressure. friendship won because i had defined the score so that friendship would win. the notation made a judgment look measured. deleting it left a more useful question: after romance became impossible, what form would preserve the relationship without crossing nicole's boundary?

the answer was friendship. i mean that as a choice about this relationship, rather than a law about love.

## distance

in the coffee shop, nicole paused halfway through a story. she was deciding whether to tell me the thing underneath it. i wanted to fill the silence. i waited.

the pause mattered because i did not already know its meaning. i could pay attention, offer safety, and still leave the decision with her. closeness helped me notice the pause. distance kept me from treating it as mine to resolve.

gadamer's account of understanding gives me language for this. two people bring different histories to a conversation, and each can revise a prior view without merging into one perspective.[@gadamer1960truth] the distance between them makes interpretation possible.

romantic pursuit would have changed that distance after she had stated a boundary. every kind gesture could have become evidence in a case she had already declined to hear. friendship let care remain care.

## shared language

over time we built shorthand for states that once took several sentences to explain. a phrase could carry a remembered conversation, a joke, and a warning about how to respond.

this shared language did not prove compatibility. it showed accumulated attention. it also required maintenance. a private reference can stop fitting the person who first helped make it. the respectful move is to ask again.

cavell's distinction between knowledge and acknowledgment matters here.[@cavell1979claim] knowing someone's habits can help me predict them. acknowledgment means letting them answer for themself. the second act sets a limit on the first.

## support

i wanted the relationship to help each of us become more capable of living a chosen life. that standard is simple enough to state and hard enough to apply.

support may mean reading a draft, making food, sharing a contact, or sitting through a bad night. it can also mean accepting that the life they choose has less room for you. care becomes control when help is offered as a debt, or when support is used to keep the other person near.

bell hooks describes love as a practice.[@hooks2000love] the word _practice_ keeps the claim local. affection is a feeling i can report. loving conduct appears in what i repeatedly do, including how i respond when desire and another person's boundary conflict.

## the boundary

the central fact was clear. she did not want a romantic relationship with me. my ache supplied no counterevidence.

friendship was possible because it did not ask her to revisit that decision each time we met. it preserved our shared language and gave both of us room to change. it also required me to stop treating patience as a strategy for receiving a different answer later.

this is where the old equation failed most clearly. a penalty term for possessiveness could name a preference. it could not show whether i had respected her. that answer lives in conduct over time.

friendship was the right form because it kept the person visible after the desired future disappeared. it was a relationship in its own right, with its own duties and pleasures. choosing it meant giving up the campaign for another form and paying attention to the one we could actually share.
