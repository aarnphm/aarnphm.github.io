## le logement

## le passé simple

you will read this tense far more often than you say it. it is the tense of written narration. [[fr/books/le-petit-prince.pdf|Le Petit Prince]] tells its story in the passé composé, the way a man talks, but its dialogue tags are passé simple: `répondit` appears 37 times, `fit` 19 times, `fut` 15 times, then `demanda` and `ajouta`. Camus wrote L'Étranger in the passé composé on purpose; Sartre said each of its sentences is an island.

four ending families cover almost every verb:

- `-er` verbs: `-a`, `-èrent` (`il parla`, `ils parlèrent`)
- most `-ir` and `-re` verbs: `-it`, `-irent` (`il finit`, `il répondit`)
- many `-oir` verbs and some others: `-ut`, `-urent` (`il put`, `il connut`)
- `venir`, `tenir` and their family: `-int`, `-inrent` (`il vint`, `ils vinrent`)

the imperfect subjunctive is its rarer twin. you meet it after a past main verb in older books: {{`Elle ne voulait pas qu'il la vît pleurer.`}}
